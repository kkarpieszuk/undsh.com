Using AI to generate text? Here you can clean it up by removing em dashes and emoji.

What is this about?

AI writing tools love using em dashes (—), while most people naturally type a regular hyphen (-). Em dashes are technically fine, but they can be a visual giveaway that the text came from AI, because many people do not even know how to type anything other than a regular hyphen. When someone spots a lot of em dashes, they often assume it is machine-written right away. Paste your generated text here and copy the "cleaned" version before you share it and accidentally look lazy.

You can also de-emojify the text - multiple emoji characters is the second most common giveaway that the text came from AI.

Tip: Instead of pasting your text in the box at the top, you can add it to the page URL right after a #.
